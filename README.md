
# 💉 Vaccine Planner Bangladesh

Enter a child's birth date and see exactly which routine EPI vaccine is due, and on which day. Works in **English and Bangla**, supports **more than one child**, and has a zone for **pregnant mothers** with a Td dose planner and a danger-signs checker.

**[Live demo](https://akshakatha16.github.io/vaccine-planner-bd/)**  

![Syringe intro animation]<img width="480" height="426" alt="intro-injection" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ea7e4155-4ad5-43fd-8e53-715e70df5c70" />

## Why

Parents often forget which vaccine comes when, or which day it falls on. This page turns a birth date into a clear, dated list that can be marked done, in the language the family reads.

## Features
- **Bangla / English** toggle, including Bangla digits and weekday names
- **Multiple children** in one browser, each with their own schedule and progress
- Date and **weekday** for every routine vaccine, calculated from the birth date
- **Mark as done / pending** for each dose, with a progress bar
- Missed doses are highlighted with advice to visit the nearest EPI centre
- **Pregnant mother's zone**: Td dose planner by vaccine history, plus a danger-signs checker with 999 and 16263 shortcuts
- **Helplines** with one-tap call: 999, 16263, 1098, 109, 333
- **Dark / light mode**, mobile-friendly, keyboard accessible, respects reduced-motion settings
- Names and dates are saved only in the visitor's own browser

## Schedule used

| Age | Vaccines |
|-----|----------|
| At birth | BCG |
| 6 weeks | Penta-1, OPV-1, PCV-1, IPV-1 |
| 10 weeks | Penta-2, OPV-2, PCV-2 |
| 14 weeks | Penta-3, OPV-3, PCV-3, IPV-2 |
| 9 months | MR-1, TCV (typhoid) |
| 15 months | MR-2 |
| 10 years, girls | HPV |
| Women 15 to 49 | Td (five doses) |

Routine EPI vaccines are free at government centres.

- Helpline numbers: public government and news pages. The 16263 service has faced funding problems, so it may not always connect.


## Disclaimer
- This is a **reminder tool, not medical advice**. Vaccination session days differ by centre, so ask your health worker which day your nearest centre vaccinates.
- The danger-signs checker is **not a diagnosis**. If something feels wrong, get care even when nothing is ticked.
- Schedules can change. Please confirm with a doctor, a health worker or your vaccine card.
